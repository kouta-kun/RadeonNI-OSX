# The installation is complete
Restart the Mac to start using the driver.
After the restart you can check what it did in Terminal:
grep RadeonNI /var/log/system.log
A good start shows the VBIOS read from the card's ROM, a mode line for your monitor, "framebuffer started" and "3D engine up".
The installer's own output is in /var/log/RadeonNI-install.log.
If you chose Open Firmware support, the Apple logo appears on the Radeon's monitor at the next start-up.
